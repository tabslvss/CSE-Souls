Most assets in this folder are CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution required). The two packs under
"Forest" marked CC BY are not: they require the credit given here and on the site's Credits page.
Sounds and particle art are listed at the end, since this file is the one list of everything.

kit-male.glb, kit-female.glb
  Universal Base Characters and Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy, by Quaternius
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
  https://quaternius.itch.io/modular-character-outfits-fantasy
  Changes: parts combined onto one skeleton, reduced polygon count, textures reduced to 256px,
  head and neck cut from the base body so they can sit above outfits.

anim-1.glb, anim-2.glb
  Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2, by Quaternius
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2
  Changes: mannequin removed, unused clips removed, tracks compressed.

weapons.glb
  PSX Weapon Pack, by Amos: https://amos-makes.itch.io/psx-weapon-pack
  LowPoly Fantasy Weapon Pack, by CardinalZebra: https://cardinalzebra.itch.io/low-poly-fantasy-weapon-pack
  Changes: converted to glTF where needed, combined into one file, textures reduced.

animals/wolf.glb, stag.glb, deer.glb, fox.glb, bull.glb
  Ultimate Animated Animal Pack, by Quaternius (CC0 1.0)
  https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html
  Changes: unused clips removed, animation tracks trimmed and resampled, geometry quantized, colors darkened.

world/forest.glb (Forest)
  Stylized Nature MegaKit, free Standard tier, by Quaternius (CC0 1.0)
  https://quaternius.itch.io/stylized-nature-megakit
  Changes: polygons reduced, leaf cards thinned, textures reduced and darkened, some leaves recolored,
  stumps and logs cut from trees.

  PSX+ Forest by ju5t1nj https://ju5t1nj.itch.io/
  PSX+ Forest Pack (CC BY, the page states no version): https://ju5t1nj.itch.io/psx-plus-forest-pack
  Used for: tree_snag, fern_psx and bark textures. tree_pine_psx, tree_oak_psx, tree_birch_psx, tree_sapling,
  rock_psx_a, bush_tall and grass_psx are in the file but not placed on the map.
  Changes: converted from FBX and TGA to glTF, rescaled, textures darkened, plant cards made two-sided.

  Low Poly dead trees, by godgoldfear (CC BY 4.0): https://godgoldfear.itch.io/low-poly-dead-trees
  tree_dead_a to tree_dead_e are in the file but not placed on the map. Credited because the file ships them.
  Changes: converted from FBX to glTF, rescaled, texture reduced to 128 px and darkened.

  Nature Kit, by Kenney (CC0 1.0): https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
  Used for: log_pile. Changes: rescaled and retextured with box-projected bark.
